TÉRRA Botanicals

TÉRRA Botanicals is a premium line of natural skincare products crafted with organic botanical ingredients. The brand was created to offer clean, effective, and sustainable beauty solutions for the modern consumer.

The project centers on making natural skincare intuitive, accessible, and enjoyable. Through carefully curated botanical formulations, TÉRRA Botanicals creates a beauty experience that is both luxurious and environmentally responsible.

I was responsible for developing the brand identity and digital experience for TÉRRA Botanicals. This included the brand strategy, visual direction, UI/UX design system, website interface, and interactive components.
