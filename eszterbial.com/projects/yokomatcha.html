Yokō Matcha

Yokō is a modern matcha brand crafted to make premium, café-style matcha accessible at home. Designed for everyday rituals, Yokō blends ceremonial-grade matcha with thoughtfully curated flavor profiles, creating a product that feels both elevated and effortless.

The brand was built to transform matcha from a niche wellness trend into an approachable, lifestyle-driven experience. Focused on clean ingredients, aesthetic packaging, and intuitive preparation, Yokō makes high-quality matcha simple, enjoyable, and visually engaging.

At its core, the project centers on redefining how matcha is experienced, not just as a drink, but as a ritual. Through cohesive packaging design and carefully curated product variations, Yokō creates a brand environment that feels calm, premium, and contemporary.

I was responsible for developing the complete brand identity and digital presence for Yokō.

This included brand positioning, visual identity design, packaging direction, UI/UX system development, website interface design, and interactive components.

The goal was to build a refined yet playful brand that balances minimalism with warmth, ensuring consistency across packaging, web design, and digital communication while creating an immersive user experience.
